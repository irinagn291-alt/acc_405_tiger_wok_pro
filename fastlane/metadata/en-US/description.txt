Take charge of a tiger chef’s counter in four illustrated night markets. Cook at different heat levels and fill customer orders before time runs out. This single-player arcade game has 16 campaign shifts with fixed order queues and 16 recipes made from eight ingredients.

Drag or tap to add ingredients to a wok. Flip dumplings and tofu to cook both sides, and transfer food between woks as needed. Browning, steam, and smoke help you judge doneness. When a ticket has arrows, ingredients must land on the plate in the order shown. Each wasted ingredient breaks your serving streak and takes three seconds off the clock during timed shifts.

Hit the serving goal to unlock the next shift. Replay unlocked shifts and earn patches by meeting their streak and waste requirements. The Recipe tin records dishes served in finished campaign attempts, even if you miss the goal. Flip a recipe card to check the cooking and plating details, then practice the dish on a single ticket with no timer.

Clear all 16 campaign shifts to unlock a 110-second Free shift with three woks and orders drawn from your learned recipes.
