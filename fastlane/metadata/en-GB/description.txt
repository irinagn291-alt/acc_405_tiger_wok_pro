Run a tiger chef’s counter across four illustrated night markets. Cook ingredients over different heats and serve customer orders before time runs out. The single-player campaign has 16 shifts with fixed queues and 16 recipes made from eight ingredients.

Drag or tap to stock a wok, flip dumplings and tofu to cook both sides, and move food between woks as it cooks. Watch for browning, steam and smoke. Some tickets show the order in which ingredients must land on the plate. Each wasted ingredient costs three seconds during timed service and breaks your serving streak.

Meet the serving target to unlock the next shift. Replay unlocked shifts and earn patches by meeting their streak and waste conditions. Dishes served in finished campaign attempts go into the Recipe tin, even when you miss the shift target. Flip a recipe card to see how to cook and plate the dish, then practise it on a single ticket with no time limit.

Clear all 16 shifts to unlock a 110-second Free shift with three woks and a queue drawn from your learned recipes.
