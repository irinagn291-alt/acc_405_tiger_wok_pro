# Tiger Wok

## What it is

Tiger Wok is a single-player arcade cooking game set in four night markets. Players manage woks at different heats, flip and transfer ingredients, and fill customer tickets before time runs out. It is for players who enjoy short, replayable timing and queue-management challenges.

## Launch and onboarding

1. On a fresh launch, the stall shows a tiger at the market, the caption “AFTER-DARK KITCHEN,” and the title “Tiger Wok.” The shift card reads “Under the Red Awning” and “First Dinner.” The controls are “Open the kitchen,” “Markets,” “Recipe tin,” and a speaker icon announced as “Sound on.”
2. “Open the kitchen” starts “First Dinner” with a “1:15” clock, a “0 / 5” score, two empty woks, and ticket “#01” for “Golden Dumpling.” The tutorial takes place at the counter. The clock holds through the first two dishes and guided cooking stops at the doneness mark for the current side.
3. “Drag a dumpling into the glowing wok, or tap the basket.” Stock “Dumpling” in the highlighted “HIGH” wok.
4. “Let the first side gild, then flick up to flip it.” Wait for the first mark to fill, then flick up or tap the occupied wok twice.
5. “When both marks are lit, swipe to the pass.” Finish the second side and send the dumpling to “PASS.” The next ticket is “Market Noodles.”
6. “Drag noodles into the hot wok.” Stock “Noodles” in “HIGH.”
7. “Sweep the noodles across to the low wok.” Transfer the noodles to “LOW.”
8. “Stock greens on high heat. Let the noodles finish gently.” Put “Greens” in “HIGH” and let both ingredients finish.
9. “Toss both cooked ingredients to the same plate.” Send the cooked “Noodles” and “Greens” to “PASS” in either order.
10. “Three more tickets. Keep an eye on the clock.” The timer starts counting down for “Golden Dumpling,” “Market Noodles,” and “Golden Dumpling.” The game opens “Shift receipt” after the last ticket or when time runs out. “First Dinner” requires all five dishes to clear.

On later cold launches the app opens at “Tiger Wok” with the first uncleared shift on the card. The primary button becomes “Take the next shift” after a campaign receipt has been recorded. After all sixteen shifts are cleared, the card shows “Free shift” and “All four markets served,” and the primary button reads “Free shift.”

## Screens

### “Tiger Wok”

- “Open the kitchen” starts the first uncleared shift when there are no recorded campaign receipts. “Take the next shift” starts the first uncleared shift once there is a record, including when the previous attempt missed its target.
- “Free shift” starts a new 110-second queue of learned dishes after all four markets are cleared. It uses three woks, two active tickets, and a next-ticket preview. The serving target is five dishes, or the number of learned dishes if fewer than five are available.
- “Markets” opens the market selector with the current campaign market selected, or “Lantern Festival” after campaign completion.
- “Recipe tin” opens the learned recipe collection.
- The speaker icon toggles gameplay sound. Its accessibility label is “Sound on” or “Sound off,” reflecting the current setting. Sound starts enabled and the choice is saved.

### Cooking counter

The heading uses the active shift name listed under “Markets,” or “Practice kitchen” or “Free shift.” Above it is the market name in uppercase. “Practice kitchen” uses “UNDER THE RED AWNING” and “Free shift” uses “LANTERN FESTIVAL.” The header shows a countdown or “∞” in practice, the served count against target such as “0 / 5,” and counters such as “STREAK 0” and “WASTE 0.”

- The back chevron, announced as “Stall,” pauses the counter and opens “Leave this shift?” with “The current shift will start again from its first ticket.” “Keep cooking” resumes the current attempt. “Back to the stall” discards the attempt and returns home.
- Ticket buttons show a customer illustration, a number such as “#01,” the dish name, and ingredient pictures. Tap a ticket to make it the destination for “PASS.” Arrows show required landing order on ordered dishes. A ticket’s accessibility value includes its plated count and ingredients, for example “0 of 2 on the plate. Rice → Tofu.” The first market has one active ticket at a time. Later markets have two. Festival shifts and “Free shift” also preview a queued dish with a line such as “Next ticket · Flash Greens.”
- Ingredient basket buttons are “Dumpling,” “Greens,” “Noodles,” “Mushrooms,” “Tofu,” “Crisp dumpling,” “Ginger noodles,” and “Rice.” The selection varies by shift. Tap to stock an available wok, or drag to an empty one. Tap an empty wok first to choose it for the next basket tap.
- Woks are visibly marked “LOW,” “HIGH,” and, from “By the Ferry” onward, “MEDIUM.” Their accessibility labels are “Wok 1, Low heat,” “Wok 2, High heat,” and “Wok 3, Medium heat.” Tap an occupied wok to select it. Tap it again to flip, or tap another empty wok to transfer. A short upward flick also flips. Drag or sweep across to transfer. Each wok holds one ingredient at a time. Browning, steam, and marks show cooking progress. Accessibility states include “Empty,” “Incoming,” “Cooking,” “Seared,” “Ready,” and “Burnt,” with the food and side for occupied woks, for example “Dumpling · Seared · Side 1.”
- “PASS,” announced as “Pass,” shows the selected ticket number and its plated food. Swipe or toss food toward it, or tap a loaded wok and then the pass. A dish serves automatically when its last required ingredient lands correctly.
- “WASTE,” announced as “Waste,” is the discard bin. Drag food to it, or tap a loaded wok and then the bin.
- The eraser, announced as “Clear this plate,” appears for a selected ticket with food already plated. It discards those ingredients and lets the player rebuild that ticket.
- The regular hints are “Flick up to flip. Swipe to the pass, or tap the wok then its target.” and “Follow the arrows on the ticket. Food cooks only in the wok.” A completed ticket briefly shows “Order served!”

### “Shift receipt”

The back chevron, announced as “Stall,” returns home. The receipt shows the shift title and “Shift cleared,” “Target missed,” or “Practice complete.” It lists dishes served by name, discarded ingredients by name, and counts such as “Dishes served · 5,” “Ingredients discarded · 0,” and “Longest streak · 5.” Outside practice it shows “Patch earned” or “Patch condition,” followed by the shift’s streak and waste requirement, such as “Streak 5 · at most 0 waste.”

- “Repeat this queue” starts that queue from its first ticket. Repeating “First Dinner” here includes its tutorial. Repeating “Practice kitchen” keeps the same recipe and unlimited clock.
- “Open the next shift” appears after a cleared campaign shift while the campaign still has uncleared shifts. It starts the next shift in campaign order.
- Once the campaign is fully cleared, a successful campaign receipt displays “Free shift” in that position. The final shift’s button opens “Free shift.” On a replay of an earlier campaign shift, the button can read “Free shift” while opening the following campaign shift.
- Practice, “Free shift,” and a missed target have no onward button. The player can repeat the queue or return to “Stall.”

## Features

- “Markets” contains sixteen replayable shifts across “Under the Red Awning,” “By the Ferry,” “In the Rain,” and “Lantern Festival.”
- “House ticket” identifies a featured dish on each shift card. Serving the required dish count clears the shift and unlocks the next one.
- “LOW,” “HIGH,” and “MEDIUM” woks cook at different speeds. Players can use taps, drags, flicks, and tosses to stock, flip, transfer, plate, or discard food. Food heats in a wok while resting there. Moving it through the air or holding it during a drag stops it heating.
- “PASS” handles the selected ticket. More than one dish can be in progress when two tickets are open. Food lands after a visible flight, and pieces arriving at the same time are counted in release order.
- “STREAK” tracks consecutive completed dishes. “WASTE” tracks discarded ingredients. A patch requires clearing the shift, meeting its longest-streak threshold, and staying within its waste limit.
- “Best receipt” keeps the attempt with the most dishes served, breaking ties in favor of less waste. Clear status and earned patches persist independently.
- “Recipe tin” collects sixteen possible dishes, with plating instructions and doneness cues. “Practice this recipe” gives each learned dish an untimed, single-ticket practice queue.
- “Free shift” offers a bonus timed queue drawn from learned dishes after the campaign is cleared.
- “Shift receipt” shows results for campaign, practice, and free play. “Sound on” and “Sound off” control cooking, landing, service, and mistake sound effects.

## Behaviours that can look like bugs

- During “First Dinner,” only the ingredient and wok for the current lesson work at the guided stocking steps. “Check the highlighted wok and the doneness marks.” can appear when a move is blocked. Follow the highlighted basket or wok, let the first dumpling side gild before flipping, and transfer the noodles to “LOW” before stocking greens. If the dumpling is wasted during its guided flip or serve step, the lesson returns to stocking. If the noodles are discarded during the guided transfer, it returns to stocking noodles.
- The clock holds and food stops gaining heat once the current side is done during the guided steps. The countdown and normal burning behavior begin at “Three more tickets. Keep an eye on the clock.”
- A basket tap needs a free wok. A transfer also needs an empty destination. An occupied wok or a wok with an ingredient already flying into it cannot take another. Wait for the landing, move the food, or discard it to free space. A drag released away from a valid target returns the ingredient to where it came from.
- “Doneness missed. The ingredient is wasted.” means the food arrived raw or burnt. Cook a fresh ingredient until its required marks are filled. “Burnt. Send it to the waste bin.” means the food stayed on heat too long. Move it to “WASTE” and stock a fresh piece.
- “Wrong landing order. Follow the arrows on the order.” means an ingredient landed ahead of another required ingredient. “This ingredient belongs to a different order.” means the selected ticket did not need that piece. Check the selected ticket, cook a replacement, and send pieces in the pictured landing order. Rejected pieces are discarded while previously accepted pieces stay on the plate.
- “Ingredient discarded. Check the marks and the order.” can follow a bin discard or a toss to a ticket that has already closed. Each wasted ingredient resets the current streak and costs three seconds in timed play once the clock is running.
- “Clear this plate” is hidden until the selected ticket has plated food. Using it shows “Plate cleared. Start this ticket again.” Every removed piece counts as waste.
- Completed tickets disappear and the rail refills from a fixed queue. Selection moves to the first remaining ticket if the selected one closes. “Next ticket” disappears when no ticket remains to preview. Reaching the serving target can leave more tickets to cook. The shift closes when the queue is finished or time runs out.
- Later shift cards can be browsed in “Markets” but are disabled until the immediately preceding campaign shift is cleared. “Target missed” leaves that next shift locked. Use “Repeat this queue” or return to “Stall” to try again.
- “Recipe tin” starts empty. Serve a dish and reach the campaign receipt to add it, even if the shift says “Target missed.” Leaving the shift before its receipt leaves its dishes unrecorded. Practice and “Free shift” do not change campaign records.
- “Back to the stall” and “Repeat this queue” reset that attempt to its first ticket, its starting clock, empty woks, and zero current score, waste, and streak. “Keep cooking” continues the paused attempt.

## Starter content and resume

The game includes sixteen preset campaign queues, sixteen dish recipes, and a guided first shift. A fresh installation starts with “First Dinner” unlocked, an empty “Recipe tin,” no receipts or patches, and sound enabled. The first two tutorial dishes are “Golden Dumpling” and “Market Noodles.”

An in-progress attempt pauses while the app is inactive and can continue when the app is brought back with the session still in memory. A cold relaunch returns to the stall. Campaign clears, best receipts, earned patches, learned recipes, and the sound setting persist. Wok contents, plated ingredients, current tickets, and time remaining are not saved across a cold relaunch.

## Platform

iPhone and iPad, with iOS or iPadOS 17.0 or later. iPhone is portrait only. iPad supports portrait, upside-down portrait, and both landscape orientations. The stall uses a side-by-side layout when there is enough landscape space, and the wider layout gives the market selector, recipe cards, and cooking controls more room.

Gameplay is the same across regions. The interface follows the system’s app-language selection and includes UK English, US English, Arabic, and Traditional Chinese.
